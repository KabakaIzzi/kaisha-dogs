Led by Experts.

Kaisha Dog Trainers was founded by Michael Wopo, a former Ugandan police officer who trained at the prestigious Israel Army K9 Institute. His deep expertise in dog handling, breeding, and behavioral training is the foundation of our work.

We don't just train dogs—we build lasting bonds between dogs and their owners. Whether you need basic obedience, security dogs, or full grooming, our team of 30+ skilled professionals is here to help.
